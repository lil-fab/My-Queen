To My Queen, Oluwaferanmi ❤️

Happy birthday, Oluwaferanmimi. ❤️

Sometimes, I think about how beautiful it is that two people can come into each other's lives and, somewhere between ordinary conversations, playful arguments, random questions, laughter, and those little moments that don't seem like much at the time, become something so meaningful to each other. When I think about us, Feranmi, I realise that some of the things that matter most are not always the big, dramatic moments. Sometimes, they are the simple things: the conversations that last longer than expected, the way you insist on knowing what's going on in my head, your little "Please rest..." moments, and how we can tease each other one minute and have a genuinely deep conversation the next. 😂❤️

And then there are the moments that go beyond laughter. The moments that show you who someone really is. For me, one of those moments was the bus issue. When things were difficult, you didn't just hear about what I was going through and leave me to deal with it alone. You called. You checked on me. You stayed concerned about how I was holding up, even when you had your own struggles and a test to prepare for. You were not feeling fine yourself, yet you still made room for me. And I will never forget that you cried with me on the phone. That moment told me something about your heart that words alone could never explain.

Today is October 22nd, and I get to celebrate one of the most beautiful people God has brought into my life. And honestly, Feranmi, I don't even know where to start, because how do you put someone who means so much to you into words without feeling like the words are somehow not enough?

But I'll try. Because you deserve to be celebrated. Not just today, but every day. And especially today, I want you to know just how much you mean to me.

You know, one of the things I have come to appreciate about you is the way you care. And I don't mean the casual, "Hope you're fine?" kind of care that people throw around because it's polite. I mean the kind where you genuinely want to know if I'm okay. The kind where you call, ask questions, listen, and sometimes refuse to let me get away with my usual "I'm fine" when there's clearly more going on. 😂❤️

And I think about the bus issue.

Feranmi, that period was not easy for me. There was so much happening, so much to think about, so much to carry, and so many things I was trying to figure out at once. I was dealing with a lot, and honestly, there were moments when everything just felt like too much.

But you were there.

You called to check on me. You kept calling to make sure I was okay. You wanted to know how I was holding up, even when there was nothing you could do to magically make the situation disappear. And I remember you crying with me on the phone.

I don't think you fully understand what that meant to me.

You didn't have to carry the weight of what I was going through, but somehow, you allowed yourself to feel it with me. You didn't just hear that I was hurting and move on with your day. My pain mattered to you. You cared enough to be affected by it.

And what gets me even more is that you were not even at your best yourself. You had your own struggles. You weren't feeling fine, and you still had a test to prepare for. You had your own responsibilities and things demanding your attention, yet you still made room to check on me and make sure I was okay.

You showed up for me when you had your own things to deal with.

And I hope you know that I don't take that lightly.

Sometimes, I get so caught up in trying to be strong for everyone, trying to solve problems, trying to make things work, trying to be the person people can depend on, that I forget that I, too, need people. People who will ask if I'm okay and genuinely wait for the answer. People who will stay on the phone with me when things are difficult. People who will remind me, without even saying it directly, that I don't have to figure everything out alone.

You have been that person for me.

And there's something you once said to me that has stayed with me. You said, "Friendship halves our sorrows and doubles our joy." Feranmi, you have no idea how delighted I was when you said that. 😂❤️ I remember feeling like I should just reach through the phone and hug you right there. Because in that moment, I felt the truth of those words. You had already been doing exactly that for me, sharing in my difficult moments, making room for my worries, and reminding me that life's burdens don't always have to be carried alone. And somehow, you put it into words so beautifully. I don't know if you remember saying it, but I do. And I think that's one of the beautiful things about you: sometimes, you say something that stays with me long after the conversation has ended.

And I am grateful. Deeply grateful.

Thank you for caring about me the way you do, Feranmi. Thank you for checking in. Thank you for worrying about me. Thank you for the calls, the conversations, the tears, the prayers, and even the times you probably wanted to shake me because I was trying to act like I could handle everything myself. 😂

Thank you for seeing me beyond the things I do and the responsibilities I carry.

Sometimes, you remind me that I'm doing something right, and you probably don't realise how much I need to hear that sometimes. You see the person behind all the things I'm trying to accomplish. You remind me that I'm still human, and that even when life gets messy, there are people who genuinely care about how I'm doing.

And you are one of those people, my love.

One of the things I appreciate most about you is your heart. Your softness, your honesty, your thoughtfulness, and the way you care deeply about people. The way you think about others, the way certain things touch you, the way you notice when someone isn't okay. You have this beautiful ability to make people feel seen, and I hope the world never makes you feel like you have to lose that part of yourself to survive in it.

Please, never lose that heart.

But also, my childdddddd, please remember that the person who is always checking on everybody deserves to be checked on too. 😂❤️ You deserve care that doesn't leave you exhausted. You deserve to be listened to, supported, protected, and loved in ways that make life feel a little less heavy.

You deserve people who will show up for you the way you show up for them.

And I pray that this new year of your life brings you more of those people. People who notice when you're quiet. People who remember to ask how your test went, whether you've eaten, whether you've rested, and whether you're actually okay. People who care about your heart as much as they enjoy your smile.

Speaking of your smile, can we talk about how much I love seeing you happy? ❤️

There is something about your smile that does something to me. It lights up my soul in a way I can't properly explain. And I hope this new year gives you countless reasons to smile. Not the kind of smile you wear because you're trying to assure everybody that you're fine, but the kind that comes from genuine happiness, peace of mind, answered prayers, and knowing that life is slowly coming together.

And please, for the love of everything, let us work on your relationship with sleep and food this year. 😭😂

Because how can you be having midnight meetings with assignments like sleep is an optional extracurricular activity? Feranmi, Please🙏. Your brain needs rest. Your body needs rest. Even your beautiful head needs to close for maintenance sometimes. 😂❤️

I want you to thrive, but I also want you to be well while you're thriving. I want you to achieve your dreams without constantly running yourself into the ground. I want you to experience the beautiful things you're working so hard for, with enough peace and strength to enjoy them.

And then there is your name.

Oluwaferanmi. God loves me.

I think there is something beautiful about that name, Oluwaferanmi. God loves me. And honestly, sometimes I look at you and think, maybe that's one of the ways He has chosen to remind me of His love. Because if God loves me, then He knew that somewhere along my journey, I would need someone like you. Someone whose presence makes difficult days a little easier, whose heart makes room for mine, and whose care reminds me that I don't always have to be strong all by myself. And He gave me you.❤️

Sometimes, I genuinely feel like one of the greatest gifts God has given me is the privilege of knowing you and having you in my life. So whenever I call your name, I hope you remember what it means. And I hope you know that, in your own beautiful way, you have become a reminder to me that God truly loves me. As you step into another year, I pray that you experience the truth of those words in ways that are impossible to ignore. May you see God's love in the opportunities that find you, the doors that open, the strength you receive when you're tired, the people who stand by you, and even the quiet moments when you need reassurance that everything will be okay.

May God give you wisdom for your decisions, grace for your journey, strength for your difficult days, and peace that doesn't depend on everything around you being perfect. May He bless the work of your hands, establish your dreams, guide your steps, and help you become everything He has created you to be.

May this year be kind to you, Mary Mercy Marvelous Elukoye. May your heart find rest. May your efforts yield beautiful results. May you never lack help when you need it, and may the love you pour into others return to you in ways that leave you overwhelmed with gratitude.

And above all, may you never forget how valuable you are, even on the days when you don't feel particularly special.

Because you are.

I celebrate your life, your heart, your growth, your dreams, your beautiful smile, and all the wonderful things that make you you. I celebrate the little girl you once were, the woman you are becoming, and the beautiful things God still has ahead of you.

Happy birthday, my Queen. ❤️
